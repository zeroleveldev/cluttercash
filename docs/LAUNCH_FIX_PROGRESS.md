## Completed bounded slice: CC-03 numeric price integrity

- Worker and Express reject non-numeric, non-finite, negative, over-limit or unordered potential prices with the existing honest 502 failure response rather than silently replacing bad prices with zero.

- Scan total getters validate each price and guard the aggregate against non-finite/unsafe values before exposing a total. They reject corrupt domain values rather than inventing a clamped valuation.
